# Third-party notices

## 5x7 bitmap font (`src/font5x7.h`)

The in-game options screen draws with the "classic" fixed-space 5x7 font from
[Adafruit-GFX-Library](https://github.com/adafruit/Adafruit-GFX-Library)
(`glcdfont.c`), transposed from its column-major form into one byte per glyph
row and embedded as printable ASCII 0x20..0x7E.
